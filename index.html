    Questions & Answers

    Here are the responses to the previous questions from your most recent emails and WhatsApp messages. I welcome your perspective on each response.

    Progress Report: How would you describe the progress achieved over the last two years?
    I had to shift a few things to find a new way to make money without getting stuck in the "rat race" or feeling like somebody's robot. The goal was to learn a new skill, tend to the food forest and just hold on until I can figure things out. When I came back from the States, I chose to bet on myself as usual. I invested in a Starlink to have real internet here and studied AI and agentic engineering every day on my own. I began building small systems, agents, and websites for friends. I built for free at first and then very cheap to pay the bills, improve my skills and get real feedback. Now I'm ready to see if that bet paid off. The plan now is to publicly offer these systems to nonprofits, social-purpose companies, and people that can use them to create good in the world and increase their impact.
    Hiring Context: Why did you hire me two years ago if the primary focus at that stage was just 'letting the garden mature'?
    Good question. I hired you at that time because I don't know what I don't know. I was very new to anything to do with raising money or even knowing how to go about starting that process. After speaking with you, I felt you were trustworthy and could help somehow. You come from that world and have a lot of experience, but in the end it was more of a feeling that it was the right thing to do. As far as letting the garden mature, I knew when starting this how long it could take on the growing side. I wanted to see what else was possible on the business and financial side of this during the process. Unfortunately, personal situations and challenges arose and I had to go home, try to work and reset. I managed to do that, but it did take away my ability to focus on that side of things.
    Transparency Gap: Why is the progression currently unclear to an observer?
    On the side of seeing an active funded project, with real students coming and going or volunteers involved, it's because it doesn't exist. It's just me here.

    The growing side of the project is called "Syntropic Agriculture" and it's doing fine. The typical timeline with no setbacks is 3 years to even see a difference.

    0 to 1.5 Years (Placenta Phase): Rapid soil cover and early biomass production.
    1.5 to 3 Years (Secondary Phase): Pioneer trees mature, and transitional crops like bananas and pineapples begin fruiting.
    3 to 15 Years: Canopy formation occurs, supporting long-term shrubs and fruit trees (e.g., citrus or coffee).
    15+ Years (Climax/Abundance): The mature ecosystem reaches deep nutrient cycling, yielding high-value timber alongside sustained crop harvests.

    We are in the three-year stage and I can walk through here and see it very clearly. What began as small bushes and a few trees is now huge clumps of bamboo, at least 200 different varieties of small plants, orchids, air plants, fruit trees, cactus, aloe, agave, bananas, mangos, limes, guavas and more.

Also, I will exhaust and contact every resource I listed in the resources section at the end of this document and track the responses.
    If Year 1 gets traction, the next step is approaching the owners about buying this property so it can always be a school. If so, we stay. If not, I've quietly built safeguards. Everything here can be extracted and moved — trees are in buried containers, most structures are built like movie props, and furniture and domes pack flat. It moves like a circus or a Super Bowl halftime show. We'd like to stay, but we're ready either way. If this becomes possible, we get the property certified as an official permaculture site and butterfly garden. That opens many doors we can touch on later as well.
    The long-term vision is to collaborate with governments and some of the larger colleges to build a reputation and increase our influence. After five years of Syntropic Agriculture, the system doesn't need to be explained anymore. Anyone who walks through it can immediately see the potential. With the right ambience, it can serve as a beautiful place to conduct interviews and invite local officials and people who care about these types of initiatives. Governments and political people always want a quick win, and with the proper alignment, many things become possible. The sky is the limit, but now we are just barely getting out of the trenches. One step at a time is how we are playing it.
    Scaling Vision: What does 'taking this model elsewhere' look like in practice?

    Take what worked in Puerto Vallarta, bring it back to Seattle, test it with youth in the community, and prove the model before trying to scale further.

    Income Velocity: Is there a faster way for you to make money online without starting a business for it?
    Short answer: yes, of course. There is no shortage of companies that want to hire people that can use AI to help them scale.

    Personal answer. I'm 47 now and 48 this year. My time is all I have and I don't feel aligned with spending it using my skills to help build and grow a corporation whose primary goal is maximizing profits at any cost and views most of their employees as expendable and replaceable. That fact is part of the reason I decided to do this project in the first place and not deal with being in the rat race or pursuing a traditional path. On a positive note yesterday I met with my first potential client to see if I could get signal or if all of this is just noise. They seemed very interested, really liked the preview and will let me know how they want to proceed. They are a new non profit as well. The biggest takeaway from that meeting was how I felt when I hung up the phone. I was genuinely happy. I really felt good having a skill that can help people, being able to answer questions honestly and not just trying to persuade someone to “buy my thing.” I felt inspired by what they were doing and it was something I can see myself doing. I'll update you when I have a response. I included a chapter in the back with examples of the offers we are making and previous projects I built and sold.
